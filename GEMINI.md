# Aviasurf

Use Aviasurf for flight search and travel discovery.

Aviasurf can:
- Find indicative flight prices for routes.
- Find filtered flight prices by dates, cabin class, baggage, direct flights, budget, and other constraints.
- Explore indicative price calendars and cheap dates.
- Compare airports serving origin and destination cities.
- Discover affordable destinations, including islands, coastal destinations, warm destinations, and mountain destinations.
- Inspect route information such as observed airlines, prices, duration, and airports.
- Resolve cities, airports, and countries to location codes.
- Open an Aviasurf live search for the user.

## Important behavior

Aviasurf provides cached indicative fare observations. These are not guaranteed live prices or confirmed availability.

When presenting indicative prices, make that limitation clear when relevant.

Use the live Aviasurf search handoff when the user wants to continue to current search results.

Aviasurf does not book flights, issue tickets, charge payment cards, cancel bookings, or process refunds. Those actions are completed with the airline or travel provider.
